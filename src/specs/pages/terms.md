## Purpose

The terms of use: the rules content has to follow. Deliberately short — three prohibitions, not a legal document. Every report reason on the [report](../components/report.md) control maps back to one of them.

## Rules

1. **No copied content** — posting another user's content as your own.
2. **No unmarked NSFW** — NSFW images and text must be flagged as NSFW. NSFW content that carries no flag breaks the terms.
3. **No illegal content** — including realistic sexual depictions of minors, real personal information, death threats, messages of violence, and degrading a real person.

### Where the line sits

Stylized or drawn depictions, including loli and shota, are not treated as literal CSAM under these terms. They are still NSFW and must be flagged as such. Realism is the dividing line: a photorealistic or convincingly realistic AI-generated sexual depiction of a minor is illegal content, and it escalates immediately instead of waiting in the ordinary review queue.
